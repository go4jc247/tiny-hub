KaiModels

Download an AI model once and save it to your Files app.

Use Wi-Fi. Keep this screen open while it downloads.
  1. Save every part to Files. Each ends in .kaimodel.
  2. Open KaiChat, tap ⚙️, then "Restore model from file".
  3. Select all the parts at once (or one at a time). Then tap Download / Load model. It loads without downloading.
